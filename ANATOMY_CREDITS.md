# Anatomy asset attribution

Lift uses modified male front/back detailed 2D anatomy SVG assets from suryamolly/muscle_mapper.

Source: https://github.com/suryamolly/muscle_mapper

The detailed advanced anatomy assets are credited by that project to Ryan Graves and are licensed under CC BY 4.0. Lift modifies the SVG presentation and maps individual source muscle surfaces into Lift's rank groups.

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The rest of Lift's anatomy renderer and styling is original Lift code.
